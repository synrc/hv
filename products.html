Products Plane (ІКС)

High-level enterprise products built on top of the Synrc VE stack.

Enterprise Resource Planning

The Products Plane (ІКС) consists of end-user business applications designed to handle specialized domains like Healthcare, Education, and Logistics. These are the consumer-facing modules of the ERP.1 ecosystem, entirely isolated from cryptographic keys and system controls, operating on top of a highly constrained capability model.
